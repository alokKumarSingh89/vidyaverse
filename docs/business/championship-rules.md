# VidyaVerse — Championship Rules

## 1. Objective

Organize fair educational competitions
that encourage learning and participation.

## 2. Event Frequency

Initial events:

- Monthly learning championships.
- Quarterly educational championships.

Future events may include weekly challenges
and annual competitions.

## 3. Initial Participants

The initial pilot shall support:

- Class 6 mathematics.
- Class 7 mathematics.
- Class 8 mathematics.

Additional subjects and educational levels
shall be introduced incrementally.

## 4. Registration

Prize championship registration shall be free.

Verify:

- Account status.
- Educational eligibility.
- Required guardian consent.
- Registration uniqueness.
- Published competition conditions.

## 5. Examination Rules

Publish before registration:

- Syllabus.
- Duration.
- Question format.
- Marks.
- Scoring policy.
- Ranking policy.
- Eligibility.
- Disconnection policy.
- Challenge policy.
- Prize conditions.

## 6. Examination Integrity

Examinations shall use:

- Immutable question versions.
- Server-authoritative timing.
- Deterministic objective scoring.
- Appropriate question randomization.
- Durable answer persistence.
- Controlled reconnection.

## 7. Ranking

Ranking shall use authoritative
Assessment Service results.

Proposed tie-breaking criteria:

1. Total marks.
2. Designated advanced question marks.
3. Designated reasoning question marks.
4. Additional verified tie-break examination.

Tie-breaking policies shall be versioned
and published before registration.

## 8. Provisional Results

Initial results shall remain provisional
until applicable verification and
challenge processes are completed.

## 9. Challenges

Participants shall be able to report:

- Incorrect questions.
- Incorrect answer keys.
- Scoring inconsistencies.
- Relevant examination disruptions.

Confirmed errors shall trigger consistent
corrections for all affected participants.

## 10. Proposed Quarterly Prizes

Per educational group:

- First position: INR 3000 educational prize value.
- Second position: INR 2000 educational prize value.
- Third position: INR 1000 educational prize value.

Initial annual reserve for three groups:
INR 72000.

Prize values are provisional.

## 11. Funding

The company shall approve and reserve
prize funding before announcing prizes.

Prize funding shall not depend on
participant entry payments.

## 12. Winner Verification

Verify applicable eligibility and
competition integrity before awarding prizes.

Automated suspicion indicators shall not
alone establish misconduct.

## 13. Release Restrictions

Valuable prizes shall remain disabled until:

- Applicable legal review is completed.
- Funding is approved.
- Questions are independently verified.
- Winner verification is operational.
- Challenge procedures are published.
- Child privacy requirements are satisfied.

## 14. Fairness

Subscription purchases shall not affect:

- Prize eligibility.
- Examination scores.
- Ranking priority.
- Competition-specific question access.

## 15. Privacy

Avoid unnecessary public disclosure
of student identities.

Obtain appropriate authorization before
publishing identifiable winner information.
