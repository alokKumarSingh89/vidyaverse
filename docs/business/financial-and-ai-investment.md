# VidyaVerse — Financial and AI Investment

## 1. Objective

Grow the platform sustainably and fund
future educational AI capabilities.

## 2. Initial Infrastructure

Use:

- Existing development hardware.
- OpenAI API.
- Cost-aware cloud infrastructure.
- Reusable educational content.
- Deterministic processing where appropriate.

Dedicated AI training hardware is deferred.

## 3. Cost Categories

Track:

- AI generation and inference.
- Document processing.
- Cloud infrastructure.
- Database and storage.
- Payment processing.
- Marketing.
- Customer support.
- Educational prizes.
- Content validation.
- Security and compliance.
- Staff expenses.

## 4. AI Cost Management

Track AI costs separately for:

- Free users.
- Paying users.
- Internal content generation.
- Document processing.
- Personalized tutoring.
- Translation.
- Evaluation.

## 5. Unit Economics

Evaluate:

- Revenue per paying student.
- Variable cost per paying student.
- Free student servicing cost.
- Contribution margin.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Subscription retention.
- Customer lifetime value.

## 6. Financial Reserves

Maintain appropriate reserves for:

- Operating expenses.
- Tax obligations.
- Refunds.
- Educational prizes.
- Infrastructure incidents.
- Future AI investment.

## 7. Future Workstation

Preferred future configuration:

- NVIDIA RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell 48 GB.
- 128 GB system memory.
- 6 TB NVMe storage.
- Compatible NVIDIA CUDA environment.
- Ubuntu LTS operating system.

Revalidate component compatibility,
availability and pricing before purchase.

## 8. Hardware Investment Conditions

Dedicated AI hardware shall be purchased
when justified by:

- Available business funds.
- Verified educational datasets.
- Measured model-training requirements.
- Expected hardware utilization.
- Cloud GPU cost comparison.
- Operational and maintenance requirements.

## 9. Model Training

Future model development may include:

- Supervised fine-tuning.
- LoRA and QLoRA.
- Multilingual educational models.
- Mathematical reasoning models.
- Document intelligence models.
- Model evaluation and serving.

Training shall comply with applicable
model licenses and dataset usage rights.

## 10. Investment Review

Review investment decisions using
measured quality, cost, revenue and
customer demand.

Revenue projections shall not be treated
as guaranteed business outcomes.
