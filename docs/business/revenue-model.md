# VidyaVerse — Revenue Model

## 1. Objective

Build a financially sustainable education platform
covering Class 1 through master's degree.

The initial pilot shall focus on Class 6–8
mathematics.

## 2. Initial Business Strategy

The initial platform shall operate without:

- Mandatory external sponsorship.
- Permanent teaching staff.
- Dedicated model-training hardware.
- Privately trained foundation models.

The initial AI provider shall be OpenAI.

## 3. Revenue Sources

Initial revenue sources:

- Student subscriptions.
- Premium educational functionality.

Future revenue sources:

- Family subscriptions.
- Additional AI usage allowances.
- Institutional subscriptions.
- Personalized mock examinations.
- Educational API licensing.
- White-label learning platforms.
- Specialized educational AI models.

## 4. Proposed Subscription Prices

- Free: INR 0 per month.
- Basic: INR 99 per month.
- Premium: INR 199 per month.
- Family: INR 399 per month.

Prices are provisional and shall be validated
against customer demand, applicable taxes
and measured operating costs.

## 5. Pricing Principles

- Maintain configurable plan versions.
- Avoid unlimited costly AI processing.
- Clearly communicate feature limits.
- Preserve academic history after cancellation.
- Disclose applicable charges before purchase.
- Separate educational purchases from prizes.

## 6. Competition Revenue Restrictions

Prize championship entry shall remain free.

Purchasing a subscription shall not provide
preferential scoring, ranking or prize eligibility.

Prize funding shall remain independent
of participant entry payments.

## 7. Business Metrics

Track:

- Monthly active students.
- Paying subscribers.
- Free-to-paid conversion.
- Monthly recurring revenue.
- Average revenue per paying customer.
- Subscription churn.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Customer lifetime value.
- AI cost per student.
- Contribution margin.
- Operating expenses.
- Operating cash flow.

## 8. Expansion

Introduce additional educational levels,
languages and subjects incrementally.

Institutional and higher education pricing
shall be evaluated separately.
