# ADR-0009: Independent Championship Funding

## Status

Accepted

## Context

VidyaVerse intends to organize educational
competitions with awards for top students.

The platform shall preserve fairness and
separate educational subscriptions from
prize competition participation.

## Decision

Prize championship entry shall remain free.

The company shall independently approve
and reserve prize funding.

Subscription purchases shall not affect
competition eligibility or rankings.

## Alternatives

- Paid prize competition entry.
- Subscription-only prize competitions.
- Participant-funded prize pools.

## Consequences

Positive:

- Equal participation opportunities.
- Clear separation of revenue and prizes.
- Predictable prize budgets.
- Educationally focused competitions.

Negative:

- Company-funded operating expenses.
- Additional participant verification.
- Potential abuse from duplicate registrations.

## Constraints

Valuable prizes shall remain disabled
until legal and operational requirements
are satisfied.

Independent question-quality approval
shall be required before valuable
prize examinations.

## Review Triggers

- Applicable regulatory changes.
- Expansion to additional jurisdictions.
- New prize categories.
- Significant competition growth.
