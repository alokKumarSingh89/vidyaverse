# VidyaVerse — Child Safety and Privacy

## 1. Objective

Protect children, adult learners and parents
through privacy-by-design principles.

## 2. Age-Aware Accounts

The platform shall distinguish between:

- Children requiring guardian involvement.
- Authorized parents or guardians.
- Independent adult learners.

Age assurance shall be proportionate
to the applicable legal requirements.

## 3. Guardian Consent

Where required, verifiable guardian consent
shall be obtained before processing child data.

Consent records shall preserve:

- Consent identifier.
- Guardian identifier.
- Student identifier.
- Policy version.
- Consent purpose.
- Verification status.
- Consent timestamp.
- Withdrawal status.

Consent withdrawal shall trigger appropriate
access and data processing restrictions.

## 4. Guardian Relationships

Guardian access shall require an authorized
relationship with the student.

Relationships shall support:

- Verification.
- Activation.
- Revocation.
- Access restrictions.
- Auditing.

A guardian role alone shall not grant
access to unrelated students.

## 5. Adult Account Transition

Students reaching the applicable age
of independent account management shall
receive an appropriate account transition.

Guardian access shall be reassessed.

Adult learner privacy preferences shall
be respected.

## 6. Data Minimization

Collect only information required for:

- Account management.
- Educational functionality.
- Security.
- Applicable legal obligations.
- Authorized business operations.

Avoid unnecessary collection of:

- Precise location.
- Biometric information.
- Government identifiers.
- Sensitive personal information.

## 7. AI Privacy

Student data shall not be used for
model training by default.

External AI providers shall receive
only necessary information.

Personal identifiers shall be removed
where practical.

Provider data handling and retention terms
shall be evaluated before integration.

## 8. AI Tutor Safety

AI tutors shall provide age-appropriate
educational interactions.

The platform shall provide mechanisms
to report inappropriate responses.

AI tutors shall not request unnecessary
personal information from children.

## 9. Advertising and Tracking

The platform shall not sell child data.

Targeted advertising directed at children
shall be prohibited.

Learning progress processing shall be limited
to legitimate educational purposes and
applicable legal requirements.

Third-party tracking shall be restricted.

## 10. Leaderboard Privacy

Public leaderboards shall avoid unnecessary
disclosure of student identity.

Use appropriate display names or
pseudonymous identifiers.

Publication of identifiable winner information
shall require appropriate authorization.

## 11. Data Retention

Retention policies shall be defined
for each data category.

Categories include:

- Account records.
- Consent records.
- Learning progress.
- Examination results.
- Uploaded documents.
- AI interactions.
- Payment records.
- Security audit records.

Retention shall consider legal obligations,
educational needs and data minimization.

## 12. User Rights

Provide appropriate mechanisms for:

- Access to personal information.
- Correction.
- Consent withdrawal.
- Account deletion.
- Data deletion.
- Grievance handling.

## 13. Security Incidents

Maintain procedures for:

- Incident identification.
- Containment.
- Investigation.
- Recovery.
- Required notifications.
- Corrective actions.

## 14. Legal Review

Applicable Indian data protection requirements,
including the Digital Personal Data Protection
Act and applicable rules, shall be reviewed
before public launch.

Child consent, age assurance, tracking,
retention and external data processing
requirements shall receive specific review.

Legal compliance shall not be assumed
from technical controls alone.
