# VidyaVerse — MVP Scope

## 1. Objective

Validate whether students and parents benefit from
AI-powered interactive learning and examination practice.

## 2. Target Market

- India.
- CBSE.
- NCERT-aligned mathematics.
- Classes 6 to 8.
- English and Hindi.
- Responsive web application.

## 3. MVP Release A — Learning Foundation

- Student and parent accounts.
- Child profiles and consent management.
- Curriculum management.
- Interactive geometry.
- Interactive arithmetic.
- Interactive fractions.
- Question bank.
- AI question generation.
- Automated question validation.
- Chapter-based practice.
- Basic learning progress.
- English and Hindi localization.
- Administrative content management.

## 4. MVP Release B — Document Learning

- PDF and image uploads.
- Question extraction.
- Extracted content correction.
- Uploaded paper practice.
- Visual answer explanations.
- Question translation.
- Basic parent dashboard.
- Provisional objective answer evaluation.

## 5. MVP Release C — Examination and Business

- Scheduled live examinations.
- Server-controlled timers.
- Answer autosave.
- Reconnection support.
- Deterministic scoring.
- Result generation.
- Class-based rankings.
- Free learning competitions.
- Subscription management.
- AI usage entitlements.
- Examination challenge workflow.

## 6. Conditional Features

Valuable prize distribution shall remain disabled
until the following are satisfied:

- Applicable legal requirements are reviewed.
- Examination questions are independently validated.
- Winner verification is available.
- Prize funding is approved.
- Child safety and privacy requirements are satisfied.

## 7. Deferred Features

- Native mobile applications.
- Full voice-based AI tutoring.
- Additional education boards.
- Additional subjects.
- Regional languages beyond Hindi.
- Advanced handwriting evaluation.
- School management.
- Teacher marketplace.
- Virtual science laboratories.
- AR and VR experiences.
- Proprietary model training.

## 8. Initial Success Targets

These are proposed pilot targets, not forecasts.

- 100 pilot students.
- 20 to 30 interactive learning concepts.
- At least 500 validated mathematics questions.
- Successful completion of one free pilot examination.
- Measurable learning improvement through pre-tests
  and post-tests.
- No unresolved critical security defects.
- No known incorrect published answer keys.

## 9. Release Principles

- Release small, complete learning experiences.
- Validate student outcomes before expansion.
- Measure AI costs and infrastructure costs.
- Avoid premature infrastructure complexity.
- Keep future features in the product backlog.
