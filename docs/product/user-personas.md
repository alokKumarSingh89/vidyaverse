# VidyaVerse — User Personas

## 1. Student

### Goals

- Understand difficult concepts.
- Practise school examination questions.
- Learn through visual experiences.
- Improve examination performance.
- Participate in educational competitions.

### Challenges

- Difficult textbook explanations.
- Limited personalized guidance.
- Fear of making mistakes.
- Language barriers.
- Limited internet connectivity.

### Product Requirements

- Interactive lessons.
- Simple explanations.
- Visual question solutions.
- Personalized practice.
- Accessible mobile-friendly interface.

## 2. Parent or Guardian

### Goals

- Understand what the child is learning.
- Help the child prepare for examinations.
- Monitor educational progress.
- Access affordable educational resources.

### Challenges

- Limited English proficiency.
- Limited technical knowledge.
- Limited time.
- Difficulty interpreting examination results.

### Product Requirements

- Independent language preference.
- Simple progress reports.
- Multiple child profiles.
- Translated learning content.
- Clear subscription information.
- Future voice-based assistance.

## 3. Platform Administrator

### Goals

- Manage educational content.
- Generate and validate questions.
- Organize examinations.
- Monitor system reliability.
- Control operating costs.
- Manage subscriptions.

### Product Requirements

- Curriculum management.
- AI generation dashboard.
- Content validation workflows.
- Examination scheduling.
- Audit logs.
- Usage and cost analytics.

## 4. Future Content Reviewer

### Goals

- Verify educational accuracy.
- Correct AI-generated explanations.
- Improve translation quality.
- Build verified educational datasets.

### Product Requirements

- Content review queues.
- Version comparison.
- Approval and rejection workflows.
- Feedback and correction tools.

## 5. Future School Administrator

### Goals

- Manage student participation.
- Organize institutional assessments.
- Monitor class performance.

### Product Requirements

- School-level accounts.
- Classroom management.
- Institutional reports.
- Assessment management.
