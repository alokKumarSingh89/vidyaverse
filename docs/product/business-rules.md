# VidyaVerse — Business Rules

## 1. Account Rules

### BR-ACC-001

A parent may manage multiple authorized child profiles.

### BR-ACC-002

A child's learning history shall remain isolated
from other child profiles.

### BR-ACC-003

Child account creation shall follow applicable
consent and privacy requirements.

## 2. Content Rules

### BR-CNT-001

AI-generated content shall begin in draft status.

### BR-CNT-002

Only content that passes required validation
may be published.

### BR-CNT-003

Published content changes shall create new versions.

### BR-CNT-004

The platform shall use original or appropriately
authorized educational material.

### BR-CNT-005

Unverified answers shall not be represented
as verified educational facts.

## 3. AI Rules

### BR-AI-001

AI providers shall be replaceable through
provider-independent interfaces.

### BR-AI-002

AI-generated structured output shall pass schema validation.

### BR-AI-003

Mathematical answers shall use deterministic
verification where supported.

### BR-AI-004

AI-generated executable code shall not be
executed in student browsers.

### BR-AI-005

Reusable generated content shall be stored
to reduce repeated AI expenses.

### BR-AI-006

Uploaded student data shall not be used for
model training by default.

## 4. Examination Rules

### BR-EXM-001

The server shall be the authoritative time source.

### BR-EXM-002

An examination shall reference an immutable
question and scoring version.

### BR-EXM-003

Objective scoring shall be deterministic.

### BR-EXM-004

Repeated submission requests shall not create
duplicate attempts or duplicate scoring.

### BR-EXM-005

Correct answers shall remain hidden until
the configured disclosure time.

### BR-EXM-006

Question corrections shall be audited.

### BR-EXM-007

An approved scoring correction shall be applied
consistently to all affected attempts.

### BR-EXM-008

AI provider failures shall not prevent students
from completing published live examinations.

## 5. Championship Rules

### BR-CHM-001

Monthly and quarterly competitions shall be configurable.

### BR-CHM-002

Prize-based competition entry shall be free.

### BR-CHM-003

Paid subscriptions shall not improve scoring
or prize eligibility.

### BR-CHM-004

Rankings shall be calculated within eligible class groups.

### BR-CHM-005

Tie-breaking rules shall be published before registration.

### BR-CHM-006

The platform shall support recognition of the
top three eligible students in each class group.

### BR-CHM-007

Prize funding shall be approved before
announcing a prize-based event.

### BR-CHM-008

Prize distribution shall occur only after
result verification and the challenge period.

### BR-CHM-009

Prize-based events shall remain disabled
until legal and operational approval.

### BR-CHM-010

The initial business shall not depend on sponsors.

## 6. Subscription Rules

### BR-SUB-001

Subscription pricing shall be configurable.

### BR-SUB-002

Free and paid plans shall have explicit entitlements.

### BR-SUB-003

Usage limits shall be enforced by backend services.

### BR-SUB-004

Subscription cancellation shall not remove
previously earned academic results.

## 7. Language Rules

### BR-LNG-001

Student and parent language preferences shall be independent.

### BR-LNG-002

Translation shall preserve mathematical meaning.

### BR-LNG-003

Translated content shall reference its original
content version.

## 8. Privacy Rules

### BR-PRV-001

Collect only necessary student information.

### BR-PRV-002

Do not sell children's personal information.

### BR-PRV-003

Do not provide targeted advertising to children.

### BR-PRV-004

Public leaderboards shall not expose unnecessary
identifying information about children.

### BR-PRV-005

Uploaded documents shall use private storage
and authorized access.

### BR-PRV-006

Personal data retention and deletion shall
follow applicable legal requirements.
