## Feature Proposal Process

Every new feature should include:

- Feature title.
- Problem statement.
- Target user.
- Expected educational value.
- Expected business value.
- Estimated complexity.
- Security and privacy impact.
- Infrastructure and AI cost impact.
- Dependencies.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Proposed priority.

New suggestions shall be recorded before implementation.

Existing requirements shall not be silently replaced.
